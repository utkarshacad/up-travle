How to Play

Each round names one of Uttar Pradesh's seven physiographic regions. Your job: figure out which districts belong to it.

1
Read the prompt. A region name and short blurb appear at the top of the side panel.
2
Guess districts. Click them on the map, or type a name and press Enter to add it. No limit on guesses before you submit.
3
Submit. Press Enter on an empty input box, or click Submit Guesses.
4
See your results highlighted on the map — see the color key below.
5
Next region. Click Next Region (or press Enter again) for a new one. Stuck? Click Reveal Solution.
